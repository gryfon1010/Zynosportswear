### 2. Google Search Console Setup (CRITICAL)
**This is the most important step!**

1. Go to: https://search.google.com/search-console
2. Click "Add Property"
3. Enter: `https://www.cgrsports.com`
4. Verify ownership using one of these methods:
   - **HTML tag** (easiest): Add the meta tag to your site
   - **DNS record**: Add TXT record to your domain
   - **Google Analytics**: If you have GA installed
   - **Google Tag Manager**: If you have GTM installed

5. Once verified:
   - Submit your sitemap: `https://www.cgrsports.com/sitemap.xml`
   - Request indexing for your homepage
   - Check for any crawl errors

### 3. Verify Your Files Are Accessible
Test these URLs in your browser:
- https://www.cgrsports.com/robots.txt (should show the robots file)
- https://www.cgrsports.com/sitemap.xml (should show XML sitemap)
- https://www.cgrsports.com/images/cgr-logo.png (should show your logo)

### 4. Test Structured Data
1. Go to: https://search.google.com/test/rich-results
2. Enter: `https://www.cgrsports.com`
3. Verify that "Organization" schema is detected
4. Check that the logo URL is valid

### 5. Submit to Other Search Engines
- **Bing Webmaster Tools**: https://www.bing.com/webmasters

## Common Issues:

### "Site not appearing in Google"
- Wait 2-4 weeks after submitting to Search Console
- Check if site is indexed: Search `site:cgrsports.com` in Google
- Verify robots.txt isn't blocking Google
- Check for manual penalties in Search Console

### "Logo not showing"
- Ensure logo is at least 112x112 pixels (yours is 513x513 ✓)
- Verify structured data is correct
- Wait 2-8 weeks after indexing
- Site must be verified in Search Console

### "Low rankings"
- Build quality backlinks
- Improve content quality and quantity
- Optimize page speed
- Ensure mobile-friendliness
- Use relevant keywords naturally

## Need Help?

If after 4 weeks your site still isn't indexed:
1. Check Search Console for errors
2. Verify robots.txt isn't blocking crawlers
3. Ensure site is accessible (not password protected)
4. Check for duplicate content issues
5. Verify hosting/DNS is working correctly
